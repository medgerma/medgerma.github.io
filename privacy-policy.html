Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game BoltSort: Nuts & Bolts Sort ("the App"), developed by MEDGERMA ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect, or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. The App does not require an account or sign-in.

The following game data is stored only on your device: your current level, coins, stars, sound and vibration settings, and your privacy (consent) choices. We do not receive this data. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. The App shows banner ads, full-screen ads between levels, and optional rewarded ads (for example, to get an extra bolt or double coins). Watching a rewarded ad is always your choice.

To show ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location (country or city)
  • Device information, such as model, operating system version, and language
  • Interactions with ads (views, taps) and app usage data, such as app launches
  • Diagnostic and performance data

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. All data is encrypted in transit. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown and before your data is used for advertising purposes. You can accept or refuse. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through the privacy settings option in the App, where it is available, or by clearing the App's data and opening the App again.

4. Controlling ads on your device

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings: Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device.

5. Virtual coins

Coins in the App are a virtual game currency only. They cannot be bought with real money, have no monetary value, and cannot be exchanged for money. The App does not contain in-app purchases.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information. The only third party that receives data from the App is Google (AdMob and the User Messaging Platform), as described above.

7. Data retention

Game data stays on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's own policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw your consent at any time. Because we do not store your personal data, requests about advertising data are best handled through Google's tools, such as My Ad Center. You may also contact us, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can help remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

2. الإعلانات (Google AdMob)

التطبيق مجاني ومدعوم بإعلانات من Google AdMob. يعرض التطبيق إعلانات شريطية (Banner)، وإعلانات بملء الشاشة بين المستويات، وإعلانات اختيارية بمكافأة (مثلًا للحصول على برغي إضافي أو مضاعفة العملات). مشاهدة الإعلان بمكافأة هي دائمًا اختيارك.

لعرض الإعلانات وقياس أدائها ومنع الاحتيال، قد يجمع Google AdMob ويعالج تلقائيًا:

  • معرّفات الجهاز، مثل معرّف الإعلانات في Android ومعرّف مجموعة التطبيقات (app set ID)
  • عنوان IP، الذي قد يُستخدم لتقدير موقعك العام (البلد أو المدينة)
  • معلومات الجهاز، مثل الطراز وإصدار النظام واللغة
  • التفاعل مع الإعلانات (المشاهدات والنقرات) وبيانات استخدام التطبيق مثل مرات فتحه
  • بيانات التشخيص والأداء

تجمع Google هذه البيانات وتعالجها، وليس نحن. جميع البيانات مشفّرة أثناء النقل. لمزيد من المعلومات راجع كيف تستخدم Google المعلومات من المواقع أو التطبيقات التي تستخدم خدماتها وسياسة خصوصية Google.

إذا كنت في المنطقة الاقتصادية الأوروبية أو المملكة المتحدة أو سويسرا، يستخدم التطبيق منصة رسائل المستخدمين من Google ليطلب موافقتك قبل عرض الإعلانات المخصّصة وقبل استخدام بياناتك لأغراض إعلانية. يمكنك القبول أو الرفض، وفي حال الرفض قد تظهر لك إعلانات غير مخصّصة.

يمكنك تغيير موافقتك أو سحبها في أي وقت من خيار إعدادات الخصوصية داخل التطبيق، حيث يكون متاحًا، أو بمسح بيانات التطبيق وفتحه من جديد.
